Privacy Policy

DealsSaver for iPhone and iPad. Last updated November 23, 2026.

Summary

The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. It does not collect, store or share personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your preferences: the state derived from the ZIP code you enter (the ZIP itself is not kept), the statements you tick, saved and hidden deals, the cars, products, states and providers you add, and the results you log.
  • These are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. "Reset app data" in the Me tab deletes all of it.

Network requests the app makes

  • Content files. The app downloads its content (deals, settlements, refund programs, repair programs and rules) from this website. The request carries no identifier and no personal data.
  • Product pictures. When a deal has a picture, it is loaded from the store that offers the deal. That store sees the picture request from your device, as any website would.
  • Recall lookups. When you add a car or a product in the Recalls section, the app queries the public databases of the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) and the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) directly from your device. Only the year, make and model, or the product words you typed, are sent.
  • Links. Tapping a deal, a claim or an official page opens that website; its own privacy policy applies there.

Purchases

Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; the app receives only Apple's confirmation of the purchase and verifies it on the device. We do not see your payment details.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no affiliate links.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No data is sold or shared.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date.
