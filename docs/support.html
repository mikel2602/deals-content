Support

Questions about DealsSaver for iPhone and iPad: eesdemir.se@gmail.com. We answer within a few days.

Purchases and subscriptions

  • Restore a purchase: Me tab → Restore purchases, or the Pro screen → Restore purchases.
  • Cancel the weekly subscription: Me tab → Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

The app has no account. Everything you enter stays on your device; Me tab → Reset app data removes all of it. See the Privacy Policy.

A deal, claim or rule looks wrong

Every entry links to the store's or the official page, which is always the final word. If something is outdated, e-mail us the entry's name and we will correct the content.
